# Future-Proofing Professionals: Flexibility and Growth Mindset in the Era of AI

Team research project (Team 07) for the IIT Madras **Strategies for Professional Growth** course, September 2025. We studied how working professionals build adaptability, continuous learning and a growth mindset to stay future-ready as AI changes their work.

## Team

Team 07: **Mohammad Sarim (Team Leader)**, Aneeqa Haroon Siddiqui, Anushka, Premshuk Saha, Watham Sabyasachi.

All members contributed to research design, interviews, analysis and the final report. My own tasks also included the team contract and research and data collection.

## Method

- **Interviews:** 5 semi-structured interviews (10–30 minutes) with professionals from different fields, including software development, government accounts, maritime navigation, clinical research and pharmaceutical research. Each used common core questions plus field-specific follow-ups.
- **Survey:** a Google Form shared through academic and professional networks, to capture broader trends.
- **Secondary research:** academic articles, reports and case studies, used to check the primary findings.

## Key findings

- **AI as a partner:** about 90% of survey respondents already use AI tools (confidence 3.92/5). Professionals use AI for routine tasks and keep judgement and responsibility for themselves.
- **Growth mindset:** professionals learned new skills through practice (survey agreement 4.31/5).
- **Systems thinking:** interviewees understood how changes in their own work affected wider operations.
- **Resilience:** setbacks were used as drivers of improvement (adaptability score 3.57/5, so moderate, with room to grow).

The reports also give recommendations for individuals, organisations and educational institutions, such as responsible AI use, human–AI collaboration guidelines, and teaching AI literacy and systems thinking.
